Car sale photo folders

Create one folder per confirmed sale listing:
images/sale/<car-id>/

Use these exact filename patterns:
exterior-1.webp, exterior-2.webp, exterior-3.webp ...
interior-1.webp, interior-2.webp, interior-3.webp ...

Photo guidance:
- Take photos in daylight with the car clean.
- Exterior: front three-quarter, side, rear, and wheels or front.
- Interior: dashboard and steering wheel, front seats, back seats, and boot or luggage space.
- Export as WebP, 1200px wide, and keep every image under 150 KB.
- Use only photos you took yourself. Do not use stock images as photos of a listed car.
- Blur all number plates before uploading.
- Add every photo path and an accurate alt description to that car's `photos` arrays in `js/sale.js`.
- Do not add a listing until its details and photos have been confirmed by the owner.
