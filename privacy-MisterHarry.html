Privacy Policy

Mitch Jhay Auto & Rentals is committed to handling personal information responsibly and only as needed to respond to rental inquiries, arrange bookings, and provide customer support.

Information we may collect

We may collect contact information you provide when you send a booking request, including your name, phone number, email, rental dates, pickup and drop-off locations, and any message details that help us understand your request.

If you book a rental without a driver (self-drive), we also ask for your driver's licence number. We ask for it only to confirm that the renter holds a driving licence for the car they will drive. If you book with a driver, we do not ask for a licence number.

For car sales, we may also receive your preferred viewing date and time, the car you enquire about, and any message you provide. Car sale enquiries do not ask you to submit identity or licence numbers.

How we use information

  • To respond to rental requests and confirm availability.
  • To confirm that a self-drive renter holds a driver's licence.
  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp, or email about your booking.
  • To arrange pickup, drop-off, and rental logistics.
  • To respond to enquiries about cars for sale and arrange a viewing.
  • To maintain basic business records as needed.

Driver's licence number

We collect a driver's licence number only for self-drive rentals (when you choose to rent without a driver). We ask for it to confirm that the renter holds a licence for the car they will drive.

The number is placed only in the WhatsApp message or email message that you choose to send to the business. This website does not save the number, and it is not stored in your browser, written to a log, or added to a web address.

Data storage and retention

Booking requests are handled through a simple static website flow and may be forwarded by WhatsApp or email. An optional configured endpoint, if the owner enables one, receives only your name, phone number, rental dates, locations, vehicle, driver option and message; the driver's licence number is never sent to it. This site does not use cookies or trackers by default. Retention periods should be confirmed with the owner and reviewed as the business grows.

Third-party services

Where we use WhatsApp, email, or a configured booking endpoint, those external channels may process your message according to their own privacy and security practices. This website does not enable advertising or tracking cookies by default.

Your rights

You may ask to review or correct the personal information you have shared with us, and you may ask us to stop using your information for future contact where applicable. Please contact the business directly to request this.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact Mitch Jhay Auto & Rentals via WhatsApp, phone, or email listed on the main site.
