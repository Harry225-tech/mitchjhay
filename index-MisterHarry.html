FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book in advance?

It helps to book early, especially for weekends and busy travel periods. We still accept short-notice requests when availability allows.

Can I rent with a driver?

Yes. You can choose a rental with or without a driver. We will confirm the option and availability when you request the booking.

Do you offer airport pickup or delivery?

Yes, location arrangements can be discussed when you send your booking request.

What do I need to bring?

Bring a valid driver's licence and any ID or deposit requirements discussed with the owner before the rental begins.

Can I book for a few days or a month?

Yes. We offer flexible rentals for short stays, business use and longer arrangements.

What if my trip changes?

Send us the updated dates or location and we will confirm what can be arranged. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm with owner]

How is the final price confirmed?

We confirm the vehicle, rental period and any terms before the booking is final. Final pricing is confirmed on WhatsApp.
